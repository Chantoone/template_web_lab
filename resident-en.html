Why Research Residency?

The programme is designed as a bridge between undergraduate training, academic research, and practical industry needs. Residents work in an environment with mentors, goals, review rhythm, and clear output expectations.

Guided research

Students do not only complete assignments or short-term projects; they pursue a research problem with enough depth to become a technical report, benchmark, paper, or prototype.

Close mentoring

Residents are supported by academic mentors, follow a regular review schedule, and receive feedback on paper reading, experiment design, academic writing, and result presentation.

Partner connection

Industry or academic partners can contribute problems, data, domain expertise, deployment contexts, and further internship or research opportunities.

Research Track

The Residency focuses on the Research Track, an approximately two-year pathway for students who want to develop independent research capability, work with mentors, and pursue academically deep AI problems.

An approximately two-year pathway for residents who want to develop independent research capability in AI, machine learning, language models, vision-language reasoning, and domain generalization.

  • Read and analyze literature systematically
  • Develop hypotheses, benchmarks, and experiments
  • Write reports, papers, or research artifacts

Research Track Roadmap

Residency is not a short-term activity. Residents are expected to maintain commitment, track progress, and join regular review sessions to gradually build independent research capability.

Stage 1

Foundations and problem positioning

Study core papers, reproduce results, and identify research gaps.

Stage 2

Experimentation and extension

Develop methods and evaluation protocols, analyze errors, and compare systematically with related approaches.

Stage 3

Writing, publication, and transfer

Complete the paper and publish it at reputable conferences or journals.

Partner Roles

Research Mentor

Guide problem formulation, critique methods, and support residents in reading literature, designing experiments, and developing academic writing capability.

Industry Partner

Provide real-world problems, data, or deployment contexts, and help residents understand operational constraints and application value.

Academic Partner

Co-mentor residents, co-organize seminars, support student exchange, or expand research collaboration with domestic and international groups.
